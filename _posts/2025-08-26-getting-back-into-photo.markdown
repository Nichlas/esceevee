Recently I have been getting back into my old hobby of photography. I've been content with not having to bring any equipment and simply taking photos with my mobile. While a mobile is an excellent camera for just snapping shots, I was missing the options that you get from a real camera. The lenses, the precision of the settings. I could fight with doing some of the same on the mobile - there are apps that can enable more settings than the normal photo-app - but I like physical way you can work with mirror lens camera.

My camera is an old Canon EOS 100D with a couple of good lenses. But I've jumped on the opportunity to get an (even older) Canon EOS 5D Mark II, as I've always wanted to try full frame photography. The 5D2 is an old model, but it got very good reviews back in the day, and I imagine that the photos you can take today are not worse.
